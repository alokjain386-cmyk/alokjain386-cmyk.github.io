Delete your kalaNDR account and data

kalaNDR - Smart Vedic Calendar (kalandr.in, Android app com.kalandr.app)

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open kalaNDR and tap Me.
  2. In the Account & sync card, tap Delete my cloud data and confirm.
  3. This deletes your synced data and your sign-in account. If asked, sign out, sign in again, then repeat the delete.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email alokjain386@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my kalaNDR account". We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your name, email, profile photo link, reminders, bills, birth details, cycle entries and settings stored in the cloud. Information kept only on your device is not on our servers; remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

kalaNDR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026. Applies to the kalaNDR - Smart Vedic Calendar website (kalandr.in) and Android app.

kalaNDR is a Panchang and Hindu calendar app. This page explains what information it uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. Contact: alokjain386@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your device

By default everything you enter stays in your device's own storage and is never sent to us: your name and birth details, reminders and alarms, bills and travel dates, cycle tracker entries, language, theme, pictures and sound choices, and your chosen location. Clearing the app's data or browser data removes it.

2. Optional sign-in and sync

If you tap "Continue with Google", we use Google Sign-In (through Google Firebase Authentication) to get your name, email address and profile photo. If "Keep my reminders, profile and settings in sync" is switched on, a copy of the following is saved in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your private account ID so it appears on your other devices:

DataWhy
Name, email, profile photo (from Google)To show who is signed in and to protect your data
Reminders, alarms, bills and travel datesSync between your devices
Profile and birth details (name, date, time and place of birth), cycle tracker entries if you use themSync between your devices and personal readings
Settings (theme, language, location, Jain tradition choice)Sync between your devices

Access rules allow only you, signed in to your own account, to read or write your data. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use it for profiling. You can switch sync off at any time on the Me tab. Without signing in, nothing is uploaded.

3. Location

The app uses your device location (only if you allow it) or a city you choose to work out sunrise, moonrise and festival dates. Coordinates stay on your device; they are saved with your settings in the cloud only if you are signed in with sync on.

5. Gmail (optional, website only)

The website has an optional feature that can read your Gmail with your permission to find bill due dates. Emails are read inside your browser, text is not stored or sent to us, and you can disconnect at any time. This feature is not part of the Android app.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

You can edit or clear your details in the app at any time. To delete your cloud copy and sign-in account, use "Delete my cloud data" on the Me tab, or see how to delete your account and data. Data kept only on your device is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.

7. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Cloud data is stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. No method is perfectly secure, so please do not enter information you are not comfortable storing.

8. Children

kalaNDR is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Not advice

Panchang, readings, cards and muhurat suggestions are traditional and informational. They are not medical, legal, financial or astrological guarantees. Cycle tracker dates are estimates and not medical advice.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions or requests: alokjain386@gmail.com.

      Read my Gmail (optional)

      Read-only access. Emails are read and understood inside your browser; nothing is sent to our servers (there are none). The app never stores email text, only the dates you tick and add. Press Disconnect to revoke access at any time. The access token lasts about one hour.

      One-time setup (about 10 minutes)
      1. Open console.cloud.google.com, create a project, then APIs & Services → Library → enable Gmail API.
      2. OAuth consent screen: type External, add your own Gmail as a Test user, and keep the app in Testing.
      3. Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → type Web application. Under Authorized JavaScript origins add https://kalandr.in.
      4. Copy the Client ID, paste it above and press Connect. Google will show an "unverified app" notice because the app is yours and in testing: choose Continue.

      Works on your own website and on you and up to 100 test users. It does not work inside the Claude preview. Google requires a paid security review before the Gmail permission can be used by the general public.

        About kalaNDR

        A Panchang engine, not a date list. Tithi, paksha, nakshatra, moon shape and festival dates are calculated from the sky above the location you pick.

        Privacy Policy · Delete my account and data

        Method: sidereal positions with Lahiri ayanamsa. A day takes the tithi running at the reference time its festival rule uses: sunrise, midday, pradosh (sunset + 40 min), midnight or moonrise. If a tithi spans two days the first day is shown; a skipped tithi (kshaya) falls on the day it is skipped. Sankranti is the day the Sun changes sign in daylight, or the following day if it changes at night. Adhika months carry no festivals. Smarta-style rules are used, so Vaishnava Ekadashi, Tamil, Bengali, Malayali and other regional calendars can differ. Regional, national and community festivals follow common practice for the state or community you choose; Islamic dates are calculated and can differ by a day with local moon sighting; state festivals tied to the solar calendar (Pongal, Vishu, Bihu and others) use the Sankranti rule above. Check important dates against a published Panchang.

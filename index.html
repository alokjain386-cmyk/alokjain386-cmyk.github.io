      Method: sidereal positions with Lahiri ayanamsa. A day takes the tithi running at the reference time its festival rule uses: sunrise, midday, pradosh (sunset + 40 min), midnight or moonrise. If a tithi spans two days the first day is shown; a skipped tithi (kshaya) falls on the day it is skipped. Sankranti is the day the Sun changes sign in daylight, or the following day if it changes at night. Adhika months carry no festivals. Smarta-style rules are used, so Vaishnava Ekadashi, Tamil, Bengali, Malayali and other regional calendars can differ. Check important dates against a published Panchang.
